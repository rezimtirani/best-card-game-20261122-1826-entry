Ember Pact — a complete card duel in the browser. You face the Ashen Warden, 30 life each, with a 30-card deck of four original suits (Ember, Tide, Thorn, Star). Spend embers to strike, guard, mend or siphon; your embers grow by one every turn. The Warden announces his intent, so every turn is a puzzle: block the big hit, or race him down first. Keyboard and mouse, synthesized WebAudio sounds, no images, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click Begin the Duel, play cards by clicking them (or keys 1–7), Space ends your turn.
